## Goal
Two rival Scottish clans dispute a border marked by nine Stones. Win a round by controlling
three adjacent Stones, or any five Stones. Control a Stone by claiming it with a stronger
three-card formation than your opponent's.

## Your turn
1. Play one card from your hand in front of a Stone on your side of the border (a Stone that
   is not claimed and where you have fewer than three cards).
2. Claim any number of Stones you are entitled to (see Claims).
3. Draw one card to refill your hand (6 cards; 7 in the Tactic variant). In the Tactic
   variant you choose which deck to draw from.
If you cannot play any card you must pass. When no card can be played by either player the
round ends: the player with more Stones wins, otherwise the round is drawn (custom rule).

## Formations (strongest first)
1. Color-Run: three consecutive values of the same colour (e.g. Red 3-4-5).
2. Three of a Kind: three cards of the same value (e.g. 8-8-8).
3. Color: three cards of the same colour.
4. Run: three consecutive values of any colours.
5. Sum: anything else.
Between two formations of the same type, the higher total wins. If still tied, the player
who completed the formation first wins.

## Claims
After playing your card you may claim a Stone if your formation there is complete and
either:
- your opponent's formation is also complete and yours wins, or
- it can be proven from the cards visible on the table (board and discard) that your
  opponent can never beat your formation, whatever cards they still play there.
A claimed Stone is turned to your side and no more cards can be played there.

## Victory
A round ends immediately when a player controls three adjacent Stones or five Stones.
In a multi-round match the round winner scores 5 points and the loser scores one point per
Stone controlled. After the last round the highest total wins the match.

## Tactic variant
Tactic cards are shuffled into a separate deck and each player holds 7 cards. When drawing
you choose the Clan or the Tactic deck. You may only play a Tactic (or expansion) card if you
have not played more such cards than your opponent. A player may play only one Joker per
round. Ruses are placed face up on a discard pile next to the board after use.

## Expansion: Highland Muster (original, optional)
Three original cards in a separate Expansion deck (two copies each). They count as special
cards for the one-more-than-your-opponent rule. See the card list for their effects.

## Saving and loading
Open the Menu on the game screen and choose Save Game (or press Cmd/Ctrl+S), then give the
game a name. Saved games appear under Load Game, in the main menu and in the game menu, where
they can also be deleted. The game in progress is also saved automatically at the start of
every turn: Continue in the main menu resumes it exactly where it stopped.
On the command line, type 'save [name]', 'saves' and 'load [name|number]'.

## Interface tips
Click a card then a highlighted Stone, or drag the card onto the Stone. Use the golden Claim
button on a Stone to claim it, then click a glowing draw pile to end your turn.
Keyboard: 1-7 select a card in your hand, then 1-9 choose the Stone; C claims every Stone
you can; E or Enter ends the turn; P passes; H shows a hint; D shows the discard pile;
M mutes or restores the sound; Esc cancels the current selection; Cmd/Ctrl+S saves.
With two human players, the screen hides both hands between turns: hand over the computer
and let the next player reveal their cards.
